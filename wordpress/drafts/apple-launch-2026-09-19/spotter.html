From saved videos to logged sets

  • Build your workout library. Find saved workouts by their thumbnails, search, favorites and collections.
  • Make the workout fit. Review and correct the card, add exercises from the exercise bank, and explore swaps for your equipment.
  • Log your training. Keep exercises, reps, weights and rest together in Workout Mode, with your last session for reference.
  • Plan and see progress. Schedule your week and follow completed sessions, training volume, muscle groups and personal records.
  • Ask Pumpy. Get help with training questions, combining saved workouts and planning. Review and confirm suggested changes before they are applied.

Start free. Add Spotter Plus.

Basic includes workout saving, logging, planning and progress, with a limited library and monthly AI allowances. Plus expands your library and monthly allowances for full video reads, Pumpy, explanations, swaps and uploads. Current allowances are shown in the app.

The standard U.S. Plus price is $49.99 per year or $6.99 per month. Subscriptions renew until canceled. The purchase screen shows the final price, applicable taxes and any eligible offer. Store availability and offers may differ.

Your training, your choices

AI processing is optional. Spotter asks before sending selected content or training context to OpenAI or Google. You can turn it off and keep logging and planning manually. Export your data or delete your account from Settings. Read the privacy policy and terms of use.

AI can miss workout details or give incorrect answers. Review exercises before training. Spotter provides general fitness information, not diagnosis or treatment.

Support and feedback

Email business@quarterdeckcollective.com for account help, a bug report or beta feedback. Include your device, app version, what you expected and what happened. Do not send passwords or sensitive health information.
