What’s new

More control over AI, and Apple beta preparation

19 September 2026

  • Spotter asks before sending selected content or training context to OpenAI or Google. Review or turn off AI processing in Settings. Manual planning and logging remain available.
  • The Apple release is being prepared with Sign in with Apple, an iPhone Share extension, widgets, Live Activities, reminders and an Apple Watch companion. A small internal TestFlight beta is underway. Public beta and App Store availability have not opened yet.
  • The standard U.S. Plus price is $49.99 per year or $6.99 per month. Purchase availability and eligible offers depend on the store; the purchase screen confirms the terms.
Everything that has changed in Spotter, newest first.

Pick your own exercise, and creator codes

Version 0.14 · 18 September 2026

  • Swap or modify now has a second path: choose from the exercise bank yourself instead of asking Pumpy. Pumpy’s suggestions also get a Use this button, so a swap actually replaces the exercise.
  • The exercise picker can filter by muscle and equipment, and it is now the way to add an exercise to a saved workout, not just mid-session. Editing an exercise gets a Change link into the same picker.
  • Picking from the bank costs none of your monthly explanations and swaps.
  • Creator codes. Enter a code from a creator you follow at sign-up, on the Plus sheet or in Settings. It is saved to your account, and, where a matching offer is available and you are eligible, redeem it through the store. Saving a creator code does not by itself apply a discount. Creators with a Spotter account see their sign-ups, subscribers and what they are owed under Settings.

Plan and Progress are now one tab: Train

Version 0.13 · 16 September 2026

  • One tab for the week you meant to have and the week you had. Swipe the seven-day strip to change week; everything under it follows.
  • Today’s workout sits on a card with Start workout and Move, and the calendar marks every day: planned, trained, trained as planned, or planned and missed.
  • Under the strip: Calendar, Progress — your muscle map, sets per day, volume and the sessions you finished — and Records for totals, personal records and awards. Spotter opens on whichever you used last.
  • Tap a day to see what you did on it and open the recap, not just what was planned.

Pumpy stays with you

Version 0.12 · 6 September 2026

  • Pumpy keeps giving a quiet blink while his greeting is on screen. It pauses when you leave or cover the page, and respects Reduce Motion and Little animations.
  • “Ask Pumpy about this workout” now shows the workout attached beside your message box. It stays selected even if an older chat finishes loading afterward.

A warmer welcome, a calmer coach

Version 0.11 · 6 September 2026

  • New here? Three short, skippable steps show how to save, find your workouts and open Pumpy. Replay them from the Quick guide whenever you like.
  • Pumpy has more expression: thoughtful while planning, pleased after a workout, and relaxed against the rack when you meet him.
  • A small blink and smoother wing movement play once, then settle. Reduce Motion and the Little animations setting keep everything still.
  • Help rows expand smoothly, tips dismiss without a jump, and the introduction keeps its buttons in place between steps.

Meet your coach

Version 0.10 · 6 September 2026

  • Pumpy has a new look, with illustrated poses for getting started, planning and finishing a session.
  • A little help where you need it: short, dismissible tips for saving, exercise options, logging, planning and Pumpy’s workout picker.
  • A quiet wing movement at a finish, then stillness. Reduce Motion is respected, and Settings lets you turn Pumpy’s tips and animations off.
  • Settings → Pumpy → Quick guide brings the help back whenever you want it.
  • Saving a preference now preserves other saved settings.

Handcrafted, not generated

Version 0.9 · 2 September 2026

  • Every emoji standing in for an icon is gone. Spotter now has a drawn icon set that looks the same on an iPhone as it does on a Pixel, and that a screen reader can name.
  • Deleting a session, a collection or an exercise can be undone. Nothing is actually removed until the message offering the undo has gone.
  • When something goes wrong, Spotter says what failed and what to do about it instead of “could not do that”.
  • Fixed: a session finished in the evening could tick the wrong day on your plan if you live far enough from London. The plan now uses your own calendar day.
  • Pull to refresh tells you what it found — how many workouts came in, or that you were already up to date.
  • The Progress figures count up when you open the tab, and the numbers stop jittering as they change.
  • Every button now shows a focus ring when you tab to it with a keyboard.
  • Pumpy’s three dots move while it is thinking, instead of sitting there.
  • A quieter set of small labels throughout, and this page, a version number and a way to report something wrong at the bottom of Settings.

Swipe between tabs, and a workout worth opening in a gym

2 September 2026

  • The four tabs are one surface you can swipe between, with the titles and the tab bar moving with your finger rather than jumping when a screen swaps.
  • Workout Mode gained a rest timer you can pause, extend or skip; a “watch the clip” button for when the name of a movement is not enough; and a session summary at the end with your time, sets, volume and anything you beat.
  • The Library answers “what am I doing today” at the top of the screen, and the creator of a video is now something you can filter by.

Share straight to Spotter, and let it watch the video

2 September 2026

  • On Android, install Spotter and it appears in the share sheet: send any video to it without opening the app. On iPhone the Shortcut is still the route.
  • Some creators say the workout out loud, or write it on the screen, and put none of it in the caption. Spotter can now listen to the video, and read what is written in it.
  • Continue with Google and Continue with Apple, without being thrown out of the app to a browser and back.
  • Every screen arrives and leaves on purpose, the light colours were brought up to the AA contrast standard, and reduced-motion settings are honoured throughout.

Pumpy, the coach

1 September 2026

  • A coach that can see what you have saved, what you have planned and what you have logged. It can build a workout, add to one or plan your week — and shows you what it wants to do before anything is saved.
  • An anatomical body map: what a workout hits, and what you have hit this week.
  • Ask for a swap when the equipment is missing, the station is busy, or something hurts, and get honest trade-offs rather than a list.
  • Collections, renaming, and the ability to correct an exercise Spotter got wrong.
  • Upload a video you already saved and Spotter will listen to it.
  • History moved into Progress, which freed a tab.

The first version

31 August 2026

  • Paste a link to a TikTok, Instagram reel or YouTube video and Spotter reads the exercises, sets and reps out of it.
  • Workout Mode: one movement at a time, logging what you lift, with what you did last time already filled in.
  • A week view for planning, and a Progress tab for volume, records and streaks.
