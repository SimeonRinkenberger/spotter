Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Spotter is a small, independent app. This page says plainly what it stores and what it does not do. If anything here is unclear, email simeonrinkenberger@gmail.com.

What Spotter stores

  • Your email address and password. Handled by Supabase Auth; the password is stored only as a salted hash and is never visible to us.
  • The videos you save — the link, the creator name, the caption or description that was public on the post, and the workout extracted from it.
  • What you log — the sets, reps and weights you record in Workout Mode, plus any notes and planned days you add.
  • A personal save key so an iOS Shortcut can add videos for you. You can replace it any time from Settings.
  • Your conversations with Pumpy, the coach, so you can pick a chat up where you left it, and the changes you confirmed from them. They are private to your account.

What Spotter does not do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics scripts, no cookies beyond the sign-in session your browser needs.
  • Your library, logs and plan are private to your account and are never sold, shared, or used to train anything.
  • No health, biometric, or location data is collected. Spotter never asks for it.

Other services involved

Saving a video sends its public caption or description to an AI model (currently OpenAI, with Google Gemini and Groq as fallbacks) so the exercises can be pulled out. Only that public text is sent.

Asking Pumpy, tapping How to do this, or Swap or modify also sends an AI model what you typed plus what it needs to answer: the titles, exercises, equipment and collection names of the workouts you have saved, your planned days, and a summary of your recent sessions (how many, which muscles, how much volume). Pumpy sends a short index of that — titles, categories, equipment, collection names, your planned days — at the start of every message so it needs fewer round trips, and sends a workout's exercises only when it needs them to answer. Your email and your notes are never sent, and nothing from any other account ever is. Pumpy cannot change anything in your library until you confirm it in the app.

Data is stored with Supabase in the United States. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Video playback happens in an embedded player from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. Those players are the platforms' own and follow the platforms' privacy policies.

Videos you upload

If you upload a video yourself — the option under the link box, for creators who say the workout out loud instead of writing it down — the file goes straight from your device into private storage that only you and Spotter's server can reach. Spotter then gives Groq a link to it that expires in fifteen minutes, so Groq can transcribe the speech, and deletes the file within minutes — as soon as the transcript comes back, or as soon as the attempt fails. Anything still there after two hours is deleted by a sweep. What is kept is the transcript, on your card, where you can read and edit it. The video itself is not kept, not shared with anyone else, and never added to the shared cache below.

When Spotter reads the video itself

Some videos say the workout out loud, or write it on the screen, and put none of it in the caption. When a card comes out thin, Spotter may go and read the video the link points at. First it sends the audio to Groq to be transcribed; if nobody is speaking, it sends the video to Google to read what is written on the screen. Neither of them keeps it — the copy Google needs for the few seconds it is reading is deleted straight afterwards, every time — and Spotter never stores the video either. What Spotter keeps is the transcript and the exercises it found, nothing else. This runs only on a card that came out thin, only for links (never for a video you uploaded), and you can also ask for it yourself with the Read the video button.

Shared extraction cache

When a video is read for the first time, the resulting workout is cached under the video's public ID so the next person who saves the same video gets it instantly and no AI call is needed. That cache holds only information taken from the public post — never anything about who saved it.

Deleting your data

Removing a workout deletes it immediately. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email simeonrinkenberger@gmail.com from your account's address and it will be erased.
