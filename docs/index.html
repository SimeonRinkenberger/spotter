Day

What counts

A session is a workout you finished with at least one set logged. Two in one day count as two. Rest days are free — the week only asks for the number you set, and it starts again on Monday.

Come up one session short and a freeze covers the week, automatically and once every four weeks. It costs nothing, and you are told afterwards.

Save from your phone

On any phone — copy a video link, then tap Save workout in Spotter.

Android — install Spotter, then choose it in the video app’s share sheet.

In TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or another app, share the post’s link and choose Spotter. If needed, open More to find Spotter in the iPhone share menu.

iPhone Shortcut setup (advanced)

For direct sharing on iPhone, create a Shortcut that sends the shared URL as a POST to this address. Keep it private — it works without your password.
