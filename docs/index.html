Spotter

Save any workout video. Actually do the workout.

You save fitness reels you never come back to. Spotter reads the exercises, sets and reps out of the video, then walks you through them one at a time and remembers what you lifted.

  1. 1
    Paste a link from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube — or share straight from your phone.
  2. 2
    Get a real workout card — every exercise with its sets, reps and rest, not a wall of caption text.
  3. 3
    Train and log it — full-screen, one move at a time, tracking weight and reps as you go.
