# Scheduling field overflow — 7 September 2026

The user's iPhone screenshot shows the date field spilling past the right sheet edge. This is consistent with [WebKit 301648](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=301648), reported 29 October 2025 and confirmed still present 14 June 2026: iOS date/time inputs miscalculate 100% width when padded. Desktop WebKit does not reproduce the same bug. [MDN date inputs](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/input/date) confirms their UI is browser/OS specific.

A regular wrapper now owns the border, background and horizontal inset. The native date input has no border or padding, can shrink with min-width:0, and retains its date type, label, value, focus and picker. Focus highlights the wrapper. Minimum input height remains 48px, including an empty value. No scheduling logic or backend changes.

Validation: build and git diff --check passed; all eight simplify-harness checks passed, including scheduling validation, deduplication, failure/retry and account/navigation races. Isolated Chrome rendered the production sheet markup and CSS with the mobile viewport at 320, 375, 393, 430 and 1280px in light/dark, with empty and two populated dates. Field bounds matched the action-row bounds, input stayed inside the wrapper, sheet had no horizontal overflow, and label/focus/date editing remained intact. Screenshots are in schedule-evidence/2026-09-07. No physical iPhone or iOS native-picker verification was available; this is a targeted workaround grounded in the reported iOS bug, not a claim of reproduction on desktop.
