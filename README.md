# PocketGym — questions and problems

PocketGym (formerly Spotter) saves workout videos as workouts you can train. If something in the app is wrong or
confusing, **open an issue here** and say what you tapped and what you expected. Please don't post personal details
(email, health information) in an issue: it is public.

- Privacy policy: https://quarterdeckcollective.com/spotter/privacy/
- Terms of use: https://quarterdeckcollective.com/spotter/terms/
- Delete your account: in the app, Settings › Data & privacy › Delete account, or https://quarterdeckcollective.com/spotter/delete-account/
